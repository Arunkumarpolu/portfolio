Keyword Research Framework

Keyword research should connect search demand with useful content and the correct website architecture.

Keyword Clustering

Group related keywords based on topic and search intent to create focused content and avoid unnecessary keyword overlap.

Keyword Mapping

Map target keywords to existing or planned URLs, helping define page purpose, primary keywords, secondary terms, and content structure.

Process

Keyword Research Process

01. Understand the Business

Understand the website, services, target audience, locations, products, and business objectives.

02. Collect Keywords

Build a broad keyword list using seed terms, competitor research, search data, and related search opportunities.

03. Analyze Intent

Evaluate search intent and determine which keywords should target existing pages or require new content.

04. Map & Prioritize

Group, map, and prioritize keywords based on relevance, opportunity, competition, and business value.
