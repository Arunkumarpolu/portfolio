Expertise

Local SEO Areas I Work On

My Local SEO approach focuses on improving local relevance, discoverability and search visibility while keeping business information consistent and technically accessible.

Google Business Profile

Optimize Google Business Profile information, categories, services, business details and local relevance signals to support stronger local visibility.

Local Keyword Research

Identify location-based keywords and search-intent patterns that can support local landing pages, service pages and content opportunities.

Location Pages

Build useful location-specific pages with relevant services, local context, internal links, metadata and clear search intent.

Review & Reputation Signals

Analyze review-related local signals and identify opportunities to improve trust, consistency and the overall local search presence.

Business Citations

Review business information consistency across relevant directories and local references to reduce conflicting location signals.

Local Schema

Implement relevant structured data such as LocalBusiness and BreadcrumbList where appropriate to help search engines understand page and business information.

Local Internal Linking

Connect location, service and supporting content through contextual internal links to improve navigation and discovery.

Local Performance Tracking

Monitor local search visibility, organic traffic, indexed pages and other relevant performance signals to identify opportunities.

Local SEO Architecture

Plan scalable location and service hierarchies that make local pages easier to crawl, understand and navigate.
