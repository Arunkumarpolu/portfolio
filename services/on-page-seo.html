Expertise

On-Page SEO Areas I Work On

My on-page SEO work combines keyword research, content optimization, page structure and technical elements to improve relevance and search visibility.

Keyword Optimization

Map primary, secondary and supporting keywords to the page based on search intent, relevance and topic coverage.

Title & Heading Optimization

Improve title tags, H1s and heading structures so pages communicate their main topic clearly and consistently.

Content Optimization

Improve content structure, topical coverage, readability and search intent alignment without unnecessary keyword repetition.

Meta Tags

Optimize title tags and meta descriptions to accurately represent page content and improve search-result relevance.

Internal Linking

Build contextual internal links between related pages to improve navigation, topical relationships and discovery.

Image SEO

Review image filenames, alt text, dimensions and placement to support accessibility, relevance and page performance.

Structured Data

Add or review relevant schema implementations such as BreadcrumbList and FAQPage where appropriate.

Page Experience

Review mobile usability, content presentation and page experience factors that can affect how users interact with important landing pages.

Competitor & Content Gaps

Compare relevant pages and search results to identify missing topics, useful sections and opportunities to make content more comprehensive.

On-Page Checklist

What I Review on a Page

A practical page-level checklist covering the elements that influence relevance, usability and technical accessibility.

Search Intent Check whether the page satisfies the intent behind the target query.
Title & H1 Review the main title and H1 for clarity, relevance and alignment.
Content Structure Check headings, paragraphs, lists and section hierarchy for usability.
Keyword Placement Review natural keyword usage across important page elements.
Internal Links Identify relevant contextual links to important related pages.
Images & Alt Text Review image relevance, filenames, alt attributes and dimensions.
Schema Markup Check whether relevant structured data is implemented correctly.
Mobile Experience Review page usability and content presentation across mobile devices.
Process

My On-Page SEO Process

A structured workflow from understanding search intent to optimizing, validating and measuring page improvements.

01

Research

Analyze keywords, search intent, competitors and content opportunities for the target page.

02

Structure

Plan headings, content sections, keyword placement and internal-linking opportunities.

03

Optimize

Improve page copy, metadata, headings, links, images and structured data where required.

04

Validate

Check implementation and monitor search performance to identify further optimization opportunities.
