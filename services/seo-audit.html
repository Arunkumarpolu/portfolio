Audit Areas

What I Check During an SEO Audit

A complete audit looks beyond individual page issues and evaluates how technical, content and search-performance elements work together.

Crawling & Indexing

Review crawlability, index coverage, blocked resources, excluded URLs and other signals that affect search engine discovery and indexing.

Technical Performance

Review page performance, mobile experience, Core Web Vitals opportunities and technical implementation issues.

On-Page SEO

Check titles, meta descriptions, headings, content structure, keyword targeting, images and page-level SEO elements.

Duplicate Content

Identify duplicate or highly similar pages and review consolidation, canonicalization or redirect opportunities.

Redirects & Canonicals

Review redirect chains, broken redirects, canonical tags and URL variations to identify technical SEO risks.

Internal Linking

Analyze internal-linking patterns, orphan pages, important page accessibility and contextual relationships.

Structured Data

Review relevant schema implementations and check whether structured data is consistent with the page content.

Sitemap & Robots

Check XML sitemap coverage and robots.txt directives for potential crawling and discovery problems.

Search Performance

Use Search Console and analytics signals to identify visibility changes, indexing patterns and opportunities requiring deeper investigation.

Audit Checklist

SEO Issues I Look For

The exact audit scope can vary by website, but these are common areas I review when analyzing SEO health.

Robots.txt & Crawl Rules Check whether important pages or resources are unintentionally restricted.
Index Coverage Review indexed, excluded and problematic URL patterns.
Broken Links Identify broken internal links and pages returning unexpected responses.
Redirect Chains Find unnecessary redirect paths and incorrect redirect destinations.
Duplicate URLs Identify duplicate URL variations and pages competing for similar intent.
Missing Metadata Check missing, duplicated or poorly optimized titles and meta descriptions.
Orphan Pages Find important pages with weak or missing internal-link connections.
Schema Issues Review relevant structured data for implementation and consistency problems.
Prioritization

How I Prioritize SEO Issues

An audit should produce an actionable roadmap, not just a long list of technical observations.

High Impact

Issues that can significantly affect crawling, indexing, important pages or organic visibility.

Medium Impact

Issues that should be improved to strengthen page quality, architecture and search performance.

Opportunities

Growth opportunities such as content gaps, internal links, metadata improvements and scalable SEO enhancements.

Process

My SEO Audit Process

A structured audit process designed to move from data collection to practical recommendations.

01

Collect Data

Gather crawl data, Search Console information, analytics signals and relevant website inputs.

02

Analyze

Identify technical, content, indexing, architecture and on-page issues across the website.

03

Prioritize

Rank issues by impact, effort and business or SEO importance to create a practical action plan.

04

Validate

Recheck implemented fixes and monitor relevant search signals after changes are deployed.
