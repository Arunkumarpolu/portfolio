What I Track

SEO reporting should focus on metrics that help explain performance and guide the next optimization action.

Search Visibility

Monitor clicks, impressions, average position, CTR, ranking trends, and query-level performance.

Landing Pages

Identify pages gaining or losing organic visibility and analyze their search performance.

Indexing & Technical Data

Review indexing coverage, crawl-related issues, sitemap performance, redirects, and other technical SEO signals.

SEO Opportunities

Convert performance data into actionable opportunities such as content updates, internal linking, metadata optimization, and technical fixes.
