Project Overview

Building a Scalable Healthcare SEO Strategy

Healthcare websites can contain a large number of pages covering medical conditions, procedures, specialists, doctors, symptoms, locations and healthcare services.

The SEO approach focused on improving the discoverability, relevance and technical quality of these page types while maintaining a consistent structure that could scale across multiple locations and healthcare topics.


The workflow combined keyword research, search-intent mapping, content optimization, internal linking, structured data, technical SEO and indexation monitoring.


A key focus was creating repeatable SEO processes that could support large numbers of healthcare pages without relying entirely on manual page-by-page optimization.

Programmatic SEO

Scaling Healthcare SEO with Programmatic Structures

Programmatic SEO can help create structured page systems when many pages follow predictable search-intent patterns.

The approach begins by defining page templates, required data fields, keyword patterns, content sections, internal-linking relationships and technical SEO requirements.


Each generated page should still provide a clear purpose and useful information rather than creating large numbers of near-identical pages.


Quality control is therefore an important part of programmatic SEO. Generated pages should be reviewed for uniqueness, search intent, indexability, content quality and technical implementation.

Content Quality

Duplicate & Overlapping Content Management

Large-scale healthcare websites can create overlapping pages when similar diseases, medicines, procedures or location combinations target closely related search intents.

Potential Issues

  • Similar page topics targeting overlapping keywords
  • Repeated content structures
  • Duplicate metadata
  • Similar location pages
  • Multiple URLs targeting the same intent

SEO Actions

  • Compare search intent
  • Review page uniqueness
  • Improve page differentiation
  • Consolidate overlapping pages where appropriate
  • Redirect or canonicalize pages when technically justified

Recommendations

Large-Scale Healthcare SEO Best Practices

Build Pages Around Search Intent

Each page should have a clearly defined purpose and should target a meaningful search intent rather than simply combining keywords and locations.

Avoid Near-Duplicate Pages

Programmatic page generation should include meaningful differentiation and quality controls to avoid creating low-value or repetitive pages.

Create Strong Internal Linking

Connect related diseases, procedures, specialists, doctors and locations using relevant contextual links.

Monitor Indexation

Use Search Console and technical crawlers to identify pages that are excluded, duplicated or incorrectly indexed.

Automate Repetitive QA

Use Python, Google Sheets and Apps Script to automate repeatable SEO validation processes where appropriate.

Maintain Healthcare Content Quality

Healthcare content should be clear, responsible, useful and appropriately reviewed before publication.
